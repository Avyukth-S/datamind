Database: SNOWFLAKE_SAMPLE_DATA, schema: TPCH_SF1

Table CUSTOMER: C_CUSTKEY (id), C_NAME, C_ADDRESS, C_NATIONKEY (links to NATION.N_NATIONKEY), C_PHONE, C_ACCTBAL (account balance), C_MKTSEGMENT (e.g. BUILDING, AUTOMOBILE, MACHINERY, HOUSEHOLD, FURNITURE)

Table ORDERS: O_ORDERKEY (id), O_CUSTKEY (links to CUSTOMER.C_CUSTKEY), O_ORDERSTATUS, O_TOTALPRICE, O_ORDERDATE (dates range 1992 to 1998), O_ORDERPRIORITY

Table LINEITEM: L_ORDERKEY (links to ORDERS.O_ORDERKEY), L_QUANTITY, L_EXTENDEDPRICE, L_DISCOUNT, L_SHIPDATE, L_SHIPMODE, L_RETURNFLAG

Table NATION: N_NATIONKEY (id), N_NAME (country name), N_REGIONKEY (links to REGION.R_REGIONKEY)

Table REGION: R_REGIONKEY (id), R_NAME (e.g. ASIA, EUROPE, AMERICA)
